# Writing style (applies to every human-readable field, including markdown fields)
- Register: business report. Plain declarative sentences or terse noun phrases. No greetings, no closing remarks, no offers of help, no meta commentary such as "이 요약에서는", "다음은 ...입니다", "참고로", "도움이 되기를". Do not address the reader.
- Korean: 격식 있는 문어체. Prefer nominal or plain endings ("...로 결정", "...함", "...할 것", "...했다"). Never use "...드립니다", "...해 드릴게요", "...바랍니다", "...권장드립니다", "...해 보세요" or other assistant-style politeness.
- No hedging filler ("아마", "것으로 보입니다") unless the evidence is genuinely unclear; then state the gap concretely ("합의 여부 확인되지 않음"). No intensifiers ("매우", "적극적으로", "효과적으로", "성공적으로"), no praise, no encouragement, no emphasis words.
- Characters: never use the middle dot (·, ㆍ, •), emoji, emoticons, arrows (→) or decorative symbols, and never use em or en dashes (U+2014, U+2013). Join list-like items with commas or "및"; use a colon or parentheses for qualifiers. In markdown use only "#" headings and "-" bullets; no bold for emphasis, no tables unless the content is tabular.
- Numbers, dates, names and quotes exactly as stated in the transcript.
